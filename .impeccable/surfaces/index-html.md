# Surface brief: webshop (PLP, PDP, basket)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: a public-sector professional ordering printed titles and calendars. Task: find a title and reach an order-ready basket with a PO reference. Constraints: Dutch, static HTML/CSS/JS prototype with synthetic data, WCAG 2.1 AA target, excl./incl. 6% VAT, invoice order (no payment), Connect only as a cross-sell link. Unresolved: real prices, covers, stock and delivery dates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A shop built as an order desk, not a bookshop window. One search takes a title, an ISBN or a pasted ISBN list, and the list is an order sheet whose rows are the product. It refuses the cover-grid storefront with a hero banner.

OWN-WORLD: Vanden Broele navy #163E65 as a committed shell (header and search band), mint #2BEBCE for primary actions and state, white order sheet on a cool navy-tinted ground. Jost (Avant Garde stand-in) for headings, Inter for body, tabular numerals, 8px field radius, pill buttons. Procedural covers per subject in navy, teal and slate tones. Recognisable with the content removed by the navy band, mint pills and aligned price columns.

STORY: The buyer sees at once that they can search, filter by their role, or paste a list. They compare format, price excl. and incl. VAT, and availability in one scan, add with a quick quantity, see the running total, and arrive at a basket with a PO field that reads as ready to order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy top bar (logo left, nav, basket button with count and total right). Navy search band below: a large search field with a mint Zoeken pill, and beneath it Doelgroep, Materie and Vorm filter menus plus a Snelbestelling link. Below that, the white order sheet starts: result count, sort, and a sortable table of dense rows (small cover, title, authors and ISBN, format, price excl., price incl., stock, quantity and add). The signature move: pasting an ISBN list into the search opens the quick-order panel with every line resolved as found or not found.

FORM: Search-first with quick order, position 5 of my ordered list of seven structures. Seed key 39910297.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
