# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: the user left the choice to the builder. Plain static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step, basket state in the browser) because this is a clickable presales prototype with mock data, not a production storefront.

## Users

Professionals at Flemish local government and public services (gemeente, OCMW, politie, brandweer, provincie, notaris, and similar). They buy a specific legal, HR, finance or welfare title for their work. They often order on a cost centre or purchase order, and often reorder. Other audiences are not confirmed.

## Product Purpose

A webshop for Vanden Broele (Brugge publisher, "Innoveren met kennis") that replaces the old catalogue search. It covers the catalogue (PLP), product detail (PDP) and basket. Success means a buyer reaches the right title and an order-ready basket quickly. This presales deliverable is a clickable front-end prototype with mock data that shows the client the direction.

## Positioning

The shop is organised around how public-sector buyers think (by target group and subject), not just by title or ISBN. It is the publisher's own channel, tied to its Connect platforms.

## Operating Context

The old catalogue (catalogus.vandenbroele.be) is a Dutch advanced-search form with filters for title, ISBN/ISSN, author, materie (subject), doelgroep (target group, with sub-roles such as Dienst Financiën under Gemeente) and publicatievorm. The old search page shows no results grid, prices, images, product detail links or basket. It also carries a seasonal delivery notice (NL/FR), and the footer links Algemene Voorwaarden, Toegankelijkheid and Contact. The interface is Dutch with some French.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scope now: catalogue/PLP with search and filters, PDP, basket. Checkout, account area and order confirmation are out of scope.
- Catalogue scope confirmed: printed books and calendars. E-books, journals and Connect licences were not selected. Show Connect only as a cross-sell link from related print titles, never as a basket item.
- Commercial facts to reflect: prices shown excl. and incl. 6% VAT, a purchase order / reference field in the basket, and invoice instead of online payment.
- Not decided: stock and delivery-date display, real prices, real covers, shipping costs.
- Language: Dutch first; French is plausible and undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Existing Vanden Broele identity, extracted 2026-10-09 (see BRANDING.md and assets/): navy #163E65, mint #2BEBCE, ITC Avant Garde Gothic headings and Inter body, 8px radius, pill buttons. Logos, emblem and favicons are in assets/. Tone is professional and low-energy, aimed at businesses.

## Evidence on Hand

Scraped site copy in vandenbroele.md, brand data in BRANDING.md and vandenbroele-branding.json, logos in assets/. Not on hand, so not to be fabricated as fact: real book titles and prices, covers, authors, stock levels, customer proof. Mock content must be labelled synthetic.

## Product Principles

- Navigation follows the buyer's role and subject, not the publisher's catalogue structure.
- Price, format and availability are visible wherever a title appears.
- The path from finding a title to an order-ready basket is short, and cost-centre and VAT details are never hidden.
- The shop earns trust through precision and clarity, in keeping with a knowledge publisher for public bodies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Government buyers: the site has a "Toegankelijkheid" page, so treat WCAG 2.1 AA as the working target (not user-confirmed). Dutch language, with French likely.
